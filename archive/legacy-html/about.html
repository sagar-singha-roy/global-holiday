THE ATELIER PHILOSOPHY

More Than A Travel Agency. We Are Travel Designers.

For years, travel enthusiasts in Tripura were limited to standard commercial tour operators offering rushed group schedules, cookie-cutter hotel rooms, and indifferent support.

We established Global Holidays Tour & Travels to offer an elevated alternative: private, thoughtfully paced journeys where your comfort, taste, and peace of mind come first.

Whether planning a romantic honeymoon to the Dal Lake houseboats of Kashmir, an adrenaline-packed expedition to North Sikkim's glacial lakes, or a family retreat to Meghalaya's crystal rivers, we manage every moving part — private SUVs, premium boutique resorts, custom meal plans, and 24/7 dedicated concierge assistance.
